### Marin Software — DEAD (cautionary tale)
- Founded 2006, IPO'd on Nasdaq 2013 as a leading search-ad management platform.
- **April 2025: board approved a Plan of Dissolution and Liquidation.** Delisted from Nasdaq 26 June 2025; filed Chapter 11 on 1 July 2025.
- **Assets sold to Kaxxa Holdings (ESW Capital, Joe Liemandt) for $5.5M** — enough for full creditor recovery and a small equity distribution.
- Lesson: the pure "manage your search ads better than the platform UI" business was fully commoditized by Google's own automation.
- Sources: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250410517913/en/Marin-Software-Announces-Plan-of-Dissolution ; https://searchengineland.com/marin-software-shutting-down-454228 ; https://elevenflo.com/blog/marin-software-bankruptcy-pre-negotiated-chapter-11

### Albert.ai — absorbed

- **Acquired by Zoomd Technologies (TSXV: ZOMD) on 27 March 2022** in a cash-and-stock asset purchase.
