# Segment 01 — SMB / Self-Serve AI Ad Management Tools

Research date: 2026-09-03. Scope: software letting SMBs/mid-market create, launch and optimize paid ads (Meta, Google, TikTok, etc.) with AI/automation. 18 named companies + 5 discovered (Creatify, Arcads.ai, Omneky, Icon.com, Pencil).

Method notes: WebSearch (Crunchbase/Tracxn/PitchBook/Latka snippets, press releases, founder interviews) + WebFetch of company sites, LinkedIn company pages, Latka pages and press releases. LinkedIn individual profile pages could not be fetched (HTTP 999); founder LinkedIn URLs below are only those that surfaced verbatim in search results — otherwise "n/a". All revenue figures from Latka are estimates and labelled "est.". "n/a" = not found.

## Cross-segment observations
- Exits: AdCreative.ai → Appier ($38.7M, Feb–Mar 2025) is the only priced 2025 exit; WordStream → Gannett ($150M, 2018) remains the segment's largest; Pencil → Brandtech (2023, undisclosed). ADYOUNEED appears folded into the Appier deal.
- Failures: Icon.com (Founders Fund-backed, $12M domain) collapsed by Mar 2026; Adext AI ceased operations 2024.
- Largest recent rounds: Arcads $16M seed (Eurazeo, Dec 2025); Creatify $15.5M Series A (WndrCo/Kindred, May 2025); Alison.ai $13.3M seed (Almaz, Nov 2024). Zocket has not raised since its $3M 2022 seed despite scaling to ~134 staff.
- Bootstrapped incumbents dominate PPC tooling: Optmyzr (~$15–24M rev, $5.3B spend managed), Opteo, Bïrch/Revealbot, Madgicx.
- Segment drift: pure "SMB self-serve" players (AdYouNeed, Adline, Adsby, Kliken, Blaze) remain small (<$5M ARR est.); winners move up-market (Zocket, Hunch, Alison, Pencil, MINT) or into creative generation (Creatify, Arcads).
