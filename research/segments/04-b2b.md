# Segment 04 — B2B paid-ads automation

### Metadata.io
- Website: https://metadata.io | HQ: San Francisco, CA / Ramat Gan, Israel | Founded: 2015
- Target: Mid-market → enterprise B2B marketing teams (Zoom, Okta, ThoughtSpot, G2, Workato) | Channels: LinkedIn, Meta, Google, display
- What it does: Autonomous B2B demand-gen — builds audiences, runs multivariate paid-social experiments, kills losers and scales winners without manual campaign ops.
- Founders: **Gil Allouche — Co-founder & CEO** — https://www.linkedin.com/in/gilallouche/ — ex-VP Marketing at Qubole; built Metadata to solve his own B2B ad-ops problem. With Asaf Shilat and Yan Manevich.
- Funding: **$58M+ total**; **$40M Series B led by Next47 and Resolute Ventures (Feb 2022)**.
- Status: Independent.

### Influ2
- Website: https://www.influ2.com | HQ: New York, NY (Ukrainian origin) | Founded: 2017
- Target: Enterprise B2B / Fortune 500 revenue teams | Channels: display, social — targeted at named individuals
- What it does: **Person-based advertising** — serves ads to specific named decision-makers rather than accounts or segments, then ties ad engagement to pipeline.
- Founders: **Dmitri Lisitski — Co-founder & CEO**; Vladimir Mikhalko — Co-founder.
- Funding: **$3.4M seed** (at the time the largest seed in ABM) + **$8M Series A led by Rally Ventures**, with a personal investment from Ryan Barretto (President, Sprout Social).
- Scale: 57+ clients including Fortune 500 B2B enterprises.

### Primer (sayprimer.com)
- Website: https://www.sayprimer.com | HQ: San Francisco, CA
- Target: B2B marketers running paid ads | Channels: Meta, LinkedIn, Google
- What it does: Audience-building — resolves and enriches B2B contact lists into high-match-rate custom audiences inside the ad platforms. Deliberately narrow scope versus Metadata's full stack.
- Funding: **$12M Series A led by Craft Ventures**, with Slack Fund and Salesforce Ventures.

### RollWorks / Demandbase / 6sense (the ABM incumbents)
- **RollWorks** (NextRoll, same parent as AdRoll) is the one built natively for account-based *advertising* — display and video orchestration against target accounts is its core. Positioned for B2B companies under ~$50M ARR; roughly $13K–$60K/year.
- **Demandbase One** targets $50M–$250M ARR companies on Salesforce; roughly $40K–$250K/year.
- **6sense Revenue AI** targets $100M+ ARR companies with mature RevOps; roughly $80K–$1M+/year. Its praised differentiator is predictive intent and account scoring rather than ad delivery.
- Sources: https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/6sense-vs-demandbase-vs-rollworks-b2b-saas-b2b-2026-abm-platform-comparison ; https://intel.42agency.com/6sense-vs-demandbase-vs-rollworks/
