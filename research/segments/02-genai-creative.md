- Target: Creators, DTC brands, marketing teams, agencies | Channels: creative for Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Snap (Marketing Studio, Higgsfield Ads, Ad Multiplier)

- Scale: **~$700M annualized revenue (Sacra, July 2026)**, up from ~$200M ARR at end of 2025. **30M users across 200 countries.** Consumption/credit-based revenue.
