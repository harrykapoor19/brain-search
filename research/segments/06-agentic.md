# Segment 06 — New agentic AI ad-manager startups (2024–2026)

### MAI — the purest "AI media buyer" bet
- Website: https://mai.com (per launch coverage) | HQ: San Francisco, CA | Founded: 2025
- Target: **SMB and mid-market** ecommerce advertisers who cannot afford an agency | Channels: Google Ads (live), expanding
- What it does: AI agents that autonomously plan, launch, manage and optimize performance marketing campaigns — explicitly framed as giving SMBs the enterprise-grade ad tooling they otherwise can't buy.
- Founders: **Yuchen Wu and Jian Wang — former Google and Instacart engineers.** Ads-ranking and marketplace-scale ML pedigree.
- Funding: **$25M seed led by Kleiner Perkins (announced Oct 2025)**, with Gao Rong Capital and UpHonest Capital. Unusually large for a seed, which signals the ambition and the compute cost.
- Scale: **managing millions of dollars of Google Ads spend per month; claims 40% higher sales for ecommerce clients.**
- Status: Independent.

### Bluefish AI (adjacent — AI-search visibility, not ad buying)
- HQ: New York | Founded: 2024
- What it does: Generative Engine Optimization — helps brands influence how they appear inside ChatGPT, Google AI search, Perplexity and Amazon, and measures it. **Not a paid-ads buying tool**, but it competes for the same "where does my demand come from now" budget.
- Funding: **$20M Series A led by NEA (Aug 2025)** with Salesforce Ventures, Crane Venture Partners, Swift Ventures, Bloomberg Beta.
- Scale: **~80% of clients are Fortune 500** — Adidas, Omnicom, Tishman Speyer.
- Sources: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-raises-20m-to-power-ai-marketing-for-the-fortune-500-302534342.html ; https://www.adexchanger.com/marketers/ai-marketing-platform-bluefish-raises-19m-in-series-a-funding/

- **FreeWheel / NBCUniversal / RPA / Newton Research** (Jan 2026) — agentic buying across linear and digital video.

### First-generation autonomous media buyers — all failed
A necessary caution for anyone betting on this category: the pre-LLM generation of "autonomous AI media buyer" companies is uniformly dead.
- **Albert.ai** — assets sold to Zoomd, March 2022.
- **Adext AI** — raised at a $20M valuation in 2016, ceased operations 2024.
- **Icon (icon.com)** — Founders Fund-backed, $9.2M seed, paid $12M for the domain (Apr 2025), claimed $5M ARR and 76 staff; the autonomous "AI CMO" never worked reliably. Team vanished from LinkedIn by Feb 2026 and reporters declared it dead in March 2026. The site is now back online **selling human-made UGC ads** ("100% real / not AI") — a complete inversion of the original thesis. The company never officially announced a shutdown.
